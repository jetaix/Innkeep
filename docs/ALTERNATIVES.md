# Alternatives to Hospitable for hosts with 1–2 listings

Every commercial product below connects to Airbnb and Booking.com through the platforms' partner APIs, which gives near-real-time availability, rates and in-app messaging; none of them is open source or self-hostable. For one or two listings the cheapest credible options are Hospitable's own free Essentials tier, Guesty Lite (from $9 per listing plus 1% per reservation), Lodgify Basic (about €13–16 per month, one property only), Beds24 (about €18–24 per month) and Smoobu (€35–47 per month at list price). Hostaway does not accept single-listing hosts. On the open-source side there is no equivalent with partner-API access: the closest project is RentTools.io (MIT, iCal only), and the hotel systems QloApps, HotelDruid and miniCal keep their channel managers as paid or proprietary add-ons. Innkeep sits in the same iCal-based niche as RentTools.io, not in the partner-API category.

> Checked on 2026-11-18. Prices change, and several vendors were running Black Friday promotions on that date; list prices are shown where the page made them visible. "Not public" means the vendor does not publish it; "unverified" means it could not be confirmed on the vendor's own site.

## Notes per product

**Hospitable.** The pricing page now lists a free Essentials plan with Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com and Agoda sync, unified inbox, calendar and scheduled messages, for unlimited properties. Host covers 1 property (max 2), Professional includes 2, Mogul 3 (€87.12/mo annual). The page showed annual prices with "Save 12%"; the monthly figure of about €29 is derived from that, not displayed. Custom upsells carry a 10% processing fee on Essentials.

**Hostaway.** The pricing page is a quiz starting at "2–14 listings"; "Only have 1 listing?" leads to a waitlist page saying the product is not available for that portfolio size. No price is published. A "Black Friday, up to 50% off" banner was shown without a base price.

**Guesty (Lite, Pro, Enterprise).** Lite is for 1–3 listings and covers Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo only. The $9 + 1% plan is described as a limited-time offer on the Lite page. Pro (4+ listings, 60+ channels) and Enterprise (200+) are quote-only.

**Lodgify.** Prices were read from the interactive calculator in EUR for 1 and 2 properties; a first page load showed slightly different monthly figures (Basic €14, Starter €26), so treat them as approximate. Professional was €48 (1) / €77 (2) with a 60% yearly Black Friday discount. All plans show 0% booking fee.

**Smoobu.** Billed in EUR. Black Friday prices were €11.90 (1 unit) / €15.98 (2 units) per month on yearly billing, against the list prices above; the FAQ on the same page states the normal discounts as 10% yearly and 20% two-year. All core features, including the website builder and API access, are in every plan.

**OwnerRez.** Prices come from the calculator data embedded in the pricing page. The base price includes channel API connections to Vrbo, Airbnb, Booking.com and Google. Hosted website, Rezzy AI and other premium features are toggles that raise the price. Currency shown as "$", country not stated.

**Hostfully.** Growth is $15 and Pro $25 per property per month, both "+ platform fee" with no figure given. Growth adds a 1% payment gateway fee and a 0.85% direct booking fee. Open API and guidebooks are add-ons. Yearly billing is discounted; the amount is not stated.

**Uplisting.** The Independent Host plan (up to 4 properties) is £40 for 1–2 and £72 for 3–4, read from the page's price table. Smart locks (£4), security deposits (£4) and dynamic pricing (£8) are per-listing monthly add-ons. REST API and MCP server access are in every plan.

**Beds24.** Pay-as-you-go in EUR with no contract; totals above are computed from the listed unit rates because the calculator did not load. A handling fee applies to top-ups under €50, and a private-label booking subdomain is €19/mo. AI features are not mentioned on the pages read.

**Hosthub.** Prices are in USD and come from the pricing arrays in the page ($39 first rental, $17 for each of the next ones, 20% off annually). Pro has the same base price plus 0.7% of net booking value. The Zero Double Booking Guarantee ($150 Standard, $500 Pro) applies to API-connected channels only.

**Open source and self-hosted.** No project found offers partner-API connections to Airbnb or Booking.com; access to those APIs is granted to approved connectivity partners, which a self-hosted tool does not have.
- RentTools.io (MIT, Next.js and SQLite, Docker) is the nearest equivalent to Innkeep: iCal aggregation, cleaning schedules, reports, message templates pasted by hand. Its README recommends it for 1–3 listings and a paid channel manager above about 20.
- QloApps (OSL-3.0, about 14,500 stars, active) is a hotel PMS, booking engine and website. Its channel manager is a hosted subscription.
- HotelDruid (AGPL, version 3.0.8 of December 2025) covers B&Bs, apartments and hotels; the booking engine and channel manager are proprietary add-ons on the vendor's hosting.
- miniCal (OSL-3.0 per search results) is a hotel PMS with channel extensions; unverified, the repository page returned 404 during this check.
- pixelcrash/Sync-Rentals-Calendar (41 stars) is an iCal merger last updated in 2020. Several other small repositories (stayops, primo, openstay-pms) exist with 0 stars and were not evaluated.

## Where Innkeep fits

Stronger:
- Free and MIT-licensed, with no per-listing fee, booking commission or paid add-ons.
- Self-hosted: reservations, guest messages and revenue data stay on the host's own machine.
- Scoped to 1–2 listings: one calendar, double-booking detection, a master copy of listing details with per-channel overrides and drift tracking, revenue estimates including fees and subscriptions.
- An MCP server, which among the products above only Hospitable (Host plan) and Uplisting list.

Weaker:
- Sync is iCal in both directions, so it is not instant. Airbnb and Booking.com refresh imported calendars on their own schedule, and a double booking can occur in that window; Innkeep detects it but cannot prevent it.
- No partner API, so no in-app messaging, no pushing of listing content, and no rate or restriction sync. Replies and listing changes are drafted in Innkeep and pasted into each platform.
- The inbox depends on a webhook the host has to feed.
- No login or multi-user support yet, so it should not be exposed to the public internet as is.
- No public direct-booking website, payment processing or dynamic pricing.
- Hospitable Essentials is also free and uses the partner APIs. Innkeep's case against it rests on data ownership, self-hosting and no vendor dependency, not on features or price.
