Innkeep

Connect the extension to your own Innkeep.

Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Copy it from the Browser extension page of your Innkeep.

  • It reads your Airbnb and Booking.com pages when you open them, or during a run you start from its toolbar button.
  • It sends reservations (guest name, party size, dates, code, status, payout) and your listing's details. Not phone numbers, messages or access codes.
  • It calls none of Airbnb's or Booking.com's APIs, takes no cookies, and never clicks or types for you. It changes pages only during a run you start from its toolbar button.
  • Airbnb's terms restrict automated data collection. You use this on your own account at your own risk.
