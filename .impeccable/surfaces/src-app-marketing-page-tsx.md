# Landing page

Scope: `src/app/(marketing)/page.tsx` and the shared public chrome. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: hosts with one or two places, not technical; developers second.
Job: watch the product work, understand what it does and does not do, then install.
Action: watch the demo first; install follows.
Proof: the product itself, played as a scripted demo with example data. No testimonials or numbers exist.
Constraints: English and French; dashboard keeps its own tokens; reference for finish is ramp.com/glass; no generated images (code-led).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every booking is a slab of tinted glass, and where two overlap the colour darkens: the double booking is visible before it is explained. Refuses the dark page with a dashboard screenshot under a left-aligned headline.

OWN-WORLD: Pale mineral ground, near-black ink. Three glass colours with fixed meaning: coral for Airbnb, blue for Booking.com, green for direct. Their overlap is a deep violet, the only accent, used for the clash and for focus. Shapes are arches, pills and rounded tiles with grain, an inner highlight and a coloured cast shadow. Product material sits on white panels floating over a field of one glass colour. Display face Funnel Display at regular weight; Geist for text.

STORY: The visitor sees two calendars become one, watches a clash appear and get flagged, learns what a month pays, reads the limits stated plainly, and installs with a prompt or three commands.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred display headline with the two-arch mark above it, one line of support, two buttons (watch the demo, install). Large glass arches and pills drift at both edges on three depth planes. The demo frame, white and rounded, rises from the fold at about 80% of page width with the calendar already playing.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the demo is a scripted film in code with play, pause and scene steps; in it a blue slab slides over a coral one and the overlap turns violet as the flag appears. Motion grammar: glass drifts slowly on scroll at plane-specific speeds; slabs settle with an exponential ease-out; nothing bounces.

FORM: Glass Calendar, candidate 3 of the grounded list; seed key fa160a80. Raises: one reading per panel (six-pack), real depth planes (multiplane), each shape answered by text with white between (bazaar).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
