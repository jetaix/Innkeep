# Roadmap

Innkeep is small on purpose: a change earns its place when a host with one or
two rentals would notice it. This page says what is being worked on, what
comes after, and what will not be built. It has no dates, because one person
maintains the project and dates would be guesses.

Something missing? [Open a feature request](https://github.com/jetaix/Innkeep/issues/new/choose)
and describe your week as a host. That weighs more than a vote.

## Now

- **Hosted early access.** A version run for you, with nothing to install. The
  website takes requests; the open questions are separate accounts, where the
  data is kept, and the price.
- **A recorded product demo.** The landing page plays a demo drawn in code; a
  short recorded film of the real app comes next.

## Next

- **Separate accounts for each person.** Today one password protects a whole
  Innkeep.
- **French addresses for the public pages.** The pages exist in French, but on
  the same address as English, so search engines only see the English ones.
- **Per-night pricing and minimum stays.**

## Later

- **Partner-API adapters,** for hosts who hold their own credentials with
  Airbnb or Booking.com.

## Not planned

- **Acting on Airbnb or Booking.com unattended.** No background crawling, no
  calls to their private APIs, no automated clicking or typing, no handling of
  a host's password or session.
- **Sending replies to guests or changing prices on the platforms.** Innkeep
  tells you what to do; you do it there.
- **Features that only make sense for agencies** with dozens of listings. The
  [alternatives](docs/ALTERNATIVES.md) cover that well.
